To permanently delete a Change.org petition, contact support with the email address you used to start your petition. Self-service deletion is not currently available on the Change.org platform. Steps to delete your petition Send an update to your supporters (for petitions with 5 or more signatures): Post an update to your supporters informing them that you are deleting your petition.
