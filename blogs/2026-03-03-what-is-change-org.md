Change.org is a platform for social change where anyone can start petitions and build support for the causes they care about. More than 500 million people (as of 2026) use Change.org to share concerns, gather signatures, and advocate for change in their communities and around the world. Who owns and operates Change.org Change.org is a public benefit corporation (PBC) that is 100% nonprofit-owned by the Change.org Charitable Foundation.
