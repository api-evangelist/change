A Change.org membership is a monthly contribution that supports the platform's mission to help people everywhere create change. When you become a member, you contribute a small amount each month to help improve and maintain the free site. Change.org is a nonprofit-owned Public Benefit Corporation (PBC) , not a traditional charity.
