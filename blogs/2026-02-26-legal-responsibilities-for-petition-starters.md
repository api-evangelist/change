When you start a petition on Change.org, you take on certain legal responsibilities. This article explains the legal requirements for petition starters on the Change.org platform. Compliance with laws and regulations Petitions, and all subsequent updates, need to abide by all applicable laws and regulations, including the laws of their country as well as laws with jurisdiction over the petition starter or the petition.
