If you've signed a petition on Change.org and want to reach out to the person who started it, you have a few options to connect with them or get updates on their campaign's progress. Leave a comment on the petition The best way to contact a Change.org petition starter is by leaving a comment directly on their petition. This allows you to show your support, ask questions, or share your thoughts about the campaign.
