When you're ready to share your petition's impact with decision makers, you can download a list of your supporters from Change.org. Important : The download option is available no earlier than 24 hours after you've created your petition, and files are emailed to you in spreadsheet(CSV) format. Because opening CSV files requires spreadsheet software, we recommend using a desktop or laptop computer rather than a smartphone or tablet.
