Disabling your Change.org account is a temporary action that prevents you from signing petitions with that email address, unsubscribes you from all marketing emails and suspends your Change.org membership. Unlike permanent account deletion, disabling is reversible - you can reactivate your account later by signing another petition. Your petitions will remain active even when your account is disabled.
